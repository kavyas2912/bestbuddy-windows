# Third-party components

The MIT project license does not replace the licenses of dependencies or model weights. Cargo.lock and package-lock.json identify the pinned Rust/frontend dependencies; their upstream packages contain license notices. Native runtimes and voice/LLM weights are downloaded separately and are excluded from this source release.

- [Tauri](https://github.com/tauri-apps/tauri)
- [sherpa-onnx](https://github.com/k2-fsa/sherpa-onnx)
- [ONNX Runtime](https://github.com/microsoft/onnxruntime)
- [llama.cpp](https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp)
- [Gradle license and notices](https://github.com/gradle/gradle/blob/master/LICENSE)
- [Voice and model links](docs/CORE_0.8.0.md)

The Android source includes its existing Gradle wrapper. Preserve upstream notices and review the selected model's license before redistributing downloaded runtime/model bundles. This source publication is not a redistribution of the model pack.
