# R7 · 21 October 2026

Daily briefing is requested from the chat header or by a briefing command. It does not automatically restart at launch or from a scheduled offer. It selects up to five distinct World, National and Local articles each, and up to two per other known category. Fewer appear if current articles are unavailable. Duplicate coverage and generic page titles are excluded. Publisher names are not read with the headlines. Weather and up to five schedule entries precede news; Market Watch follows.

Quiet is available in the chat header, onboarding and Settings. It interrupts speech, cancels remaining briefing lines and suppresses automatic guidance/chatter until Talk, Daily briefing or Hear guidance is requested. It leaves microphone permissions and saved voice choices unchanged. Stale pending speech cannot resume after a later unmute. Spoken quiet, shut up, no need and stop briefing are recognised while busy.

Settings and onboarding guidance keep the Butler voice and rotate polite introductory phrases. Browser layout previews use browser speech when native TTS is unavailable; they cannot demonstrate the actual native effect. Settings pages have Hear guidance and Quiet. English remains the fallback. Android keeps its lightweight starter voice as fallback if the full speech engine is not ready.

The native composer rotates greetings, wellbeing, capabilities and clarification phrases; guidance uses the same phrase library. Each situation exhausts its choices before repeating. This is in-memory rotation for a running app, not permanent suppression of a useful phrase across every restart. News facts and explicit personal phrasebook replies are not rewritten.

Source packages include build scripts, not newly built installers. Audio quality, live feed availability and phone interruption behaviour require device testing.
