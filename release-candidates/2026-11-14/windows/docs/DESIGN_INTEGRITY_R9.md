Retest Samsung S20FE5G SM-G781B/DS: first launch, offline starter extraction, permissions, greeting, diagnostics, save/restart, keyboard/system bars, navigation, real speech interruption, quiet periods, calls/headsets/Bluetooth and notification Stop.
